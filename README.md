# Potter Trivia

<p>Welcome to the world of Harry Potter, where you get to test your knowledge of characters (wizards and muggles), magical spells, and location of events in the novels and films. Before starting you must select your house, something normally done during the sorting ceremony in the Great Hall at the Hogwarts School of Witchcraft.</p>
<p>To play the game, select one of 3 categories, Who (character), What (spell), and Where (location). Selecting one will generate a random question where you're given 3 choices, with only one of them being correct. A correct answer awards points to your house, incorrect answers give points to one of the other houses.</p>
<p>The goal of the game is to achieve the highest score of all houses. There is no time limit. To make things interesting, set a time limit when playing with friends, to see who can get the highest score. Good luck and may the best wizard or witch win!</p>
